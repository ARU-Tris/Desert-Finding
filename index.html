SPACE · REST   H · NOTE   ESC · PAUSE

Begin or Continue Journey, then click or tap a direction from the centered traveler to walk continuously. Click again to turn and correct gradual navigation drift. Space stops or resumes walking, H shows field notes, Escape pauses. The compass red needle always seeks North and its bearing is your actual walking heading. Walk at 5 kilometers per hour across a 9.2 million square kilometer desert. There is no assigned destination. Only physically crossing the natural boundary ends the journey. Progress saves on this device and advances only during active play. Sunrise is at 06:00 and sunset at 18:00 in your local time.
